---
title: "Mixed Reactions"
---

"There are so many people. ... I haven't seen anything like it since I've been alive," a visitor from Sacramento State told the Nexus.

"This party has been outrageous, we've never seen a party this big before, we've been to Chico, and Cal Poly," a visitor from San Jose said. "Chico is nothing compared to this."

"I drove 300 miles for this? I expected more people, more beer and more riots," a UCLA student said.

"The main problem is people being considerate. There's a lot of flashers," said a UCSB student and volunteer.
