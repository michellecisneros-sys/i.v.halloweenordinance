On October 29, the News-Press published an article titled "30,000 partiers expected in I.V." as a precursor to the event. The article detailed precautionary measures by local entities ahead of the event. According to the article, crowds were already expected to double from the previous year's 15,000 attendees. The staff writer hypothesized that this may have been the case because Halloween fell on a Saturday.
